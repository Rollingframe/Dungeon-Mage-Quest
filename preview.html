Dungeon Mage Quest

A render of the display after the flicker/legibility fix, plus mockups of the requested additions — kill/hit magic effects, the up / down / side control scheme, two new monsters, and the startup jingle — before any of it goes back into the sketch.

Interface, after the fix

135×240 native, rendered here at 1.25× with pixelated scaling for a true read. Every screen now shares the same stone-plaque chrome; text auto-grows when it fits.

Proposed combat effects

Looping so you can watch the timing — on real hardware each is 2–4 frames, ~120–250ms total, composited into the existing canvas-buffer/pushSprite frame.

New control scheme

Replaces the old 4-gesture set (slash/chop/thrust/block). Side-to-side becomes the one attack input; up and down split into two distinct defensive reads.

Lift up → Block

Raise the wand sharply. Stops a BLOCK-flagged attack cold and reflects a hit back.

Push down → Parry

New. A sharp downward push answers a PARRY-flagged attack — same reflect, distinct motion so you can't just wave the wand and win.

Side to side → Attack

Any horizontal or forward swing lands a hit. Slash/Chop/Thrust collapse into one legible "swing" input — obstacles now only ever ask for Attack, Block, or Parry.

Monster roster

Two new fantasy staples join the three existing species. Difficulty scales with dungeon depth — deeper floors weight the pool toward the harder ones.

Startup jingle

Monophonic square-wave, matching the M5StickC's single piezo speaker — the same eight M5.Speaker.tone(hz, ms) calls would run at boot, right before the title card.

C5 · E5 · G5 · C6 (hold) · rest · G5 · A5 · C6 (hold) — a quick rising fanfare, ~1.1s, in the spirit of an old 8-bit RPG boot chime.
